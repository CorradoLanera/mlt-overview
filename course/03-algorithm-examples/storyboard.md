## Modalità duale — dove si attiva

Il riquadro **5** è il perno attivo del capitolo: lì gira il sommativo "**Three algorithms, one dataset**" (vedi [objectives.md](objectives.md)). L'artefatto condiviso è **una sola scatter 2D non-linearmente-separabile** + una lavagna a **3 colonne** ($k$NN · SVM · RF) che presenza e remoto popolano con i loro sketch (foto di carta + drawing tool su slide condivisa) + un poll Slido sulla *forma di regione predetta* per ogni algoritmo (con 5 distrattori, non 3, per evitare il matching banale). Riquadri 1–4 espositivi (~22′), riquadro 5 ospita i ~10′ di sommativo (min 24–34), riquadro 6 chiude e fa ponte (~1–2′).

## Prompt visivi

Prompt pronti per un generatore di immagini. Lo **`style-anchor`** è definito *una volta* (stesso del cap. 01 e cap. 02, per coerenza di serie tra capitoli) e va premesso a ogni prompt come un parametro. I riquadri scrivono solo `style-anchor + <soggetto>`. Mai testo dentro l'immagine — le label matematiche (es. $\phi$, $k=3$) si aggiungono come overlay in slide-editor.

**`style-anchor`** = *"Coherent image set, one shared visual identity: warm orange (#E8741E) as the single accent colour on a dark-slate background, soft cinematic lighting, 16:9, crisp and high quality, no text, letters or numbers rendered anywhere in the image."*

- **Riquadro 1 (Hook)**: `style-anchor` + *flat data-viz scatter of two-colour patient dots on a plane, three differently-shaped dividing curves overlaid on top (a wiggly one hugging the dots, a smooth curved margin, an axis-aligned staircase pattern), all in the same warm-orange accent at varying opacities; a faint clinician silhouette glances on.*
- **Riquadro 2 (Contesto)**: `style-anchor` + *split-panel infographic: left, a single human hand drawing a straight line on a 2D scatter by pen; center, a large arrow with a question-mark shape; right, three small abstract machine icons (a chain of connected dots, a straight slanting line, a small branching tree), each emitting its own dividing curve on the same scatter.*
- **Riquadro 3 (Sfida / $k$NN)**: `style-anchor` + *a 2D scatter with two coloured clusters of patient dots; one larger unfilled test point sits in the middle; three thin orange lines connect that test point to its three nearest training dots; nearby, a wiggly boundary curve hugs the edges of each cluster.*
- **Riquadro 4 (Nodo / kernel trick)**: `style-anchor` + *three-panel sequence in a single row. Left panel: 2D scatter with two classes forming interlocked half-moons (clearly non-linearly separable), a straight line drawn across that misclassifies many dots. Middle panel: an arrow rises and lifts the same dots into a 3D perspective view, where a single flat plane now separates the two clusters cleanly. Right panel: the same 2D scatter again, with a smooth curved boundary correctly enclosing one class.*
- **Riquadro 5 (Metodo / ensembles + 3-column board)**: `style-anchor` + *top half: a vertical column of many small decision trees, each emitting a plus or minus vote, all funnelling into a single combined result; next to it a small monotonic line chart climbing from low to high. Bottom half: a 3-column board, each column labelled (k-NN | SVM | RF) showing a single distinct boundary shape on the same 2D scatter — a wiggly piecewise curve, a smooth curved margin, an axis-aligned staircase made of small rectangles.*
- **Riquadro 6 (Payoff)**: `style-anchor` + *center: a tight wiggly boundary that wraps every training dot with paper-thin precision (each dot inside its own correctly-coloured bubble); to the right, a single new unfilled dot floats just outside the wrap and lands in the wrong colour region; behind, an open empty horizon, suggesting the unseen patients to come.*
