Item 02 — Long-range dependency on a clinical sentence: RNN traversal vs Transformer self-attention

ID: IB-07-llm-transformers-02 Learning objective: Contrast the Transformer's self-attention with the RNN's sequential hidden state on a sequence of length TxT_x — identify a long-range dependency in a clinical sentence, quantify how many intermediate hidden states an RNN must traverse to propagate that dependency, and locate the single entry of the QK⊤Q K^{\top} attention matrix that captures the same dependency in one step (Obj. 2).

Category: Application Type: applicativa Difficulty: medium Chapter: 07-llm-transformers

Question

Consider the following clinical sentence, taken from a fictional discharge letter. The words have been numbered so you can refer to positions unambiguously.

The1_{1} discharge2_{2} summary3_{3} explicitly4_{4} rules5_{5} out6_{6} atrial7_{7} fibrillation8_{8}, although9_{9} the10_{10} 48-hour11_{11} Holter12_{12} recording13_{13} from14_{14} the15_{15} prior16_{16} week17_{17} — attached18_{18} as19_{19} appendix20_{20} C21_{21} and22_{22} reviewed23_{23} only24_{24} by25_{25} the26_{26} on-call27_{27} cardiologist28_{28} this29_{29} afternoon30_{30} — demonstrates31_{31} paroxysmal32_{32} episodes33_{33}.

The sentence's total length, after this stylised word-level tokenisation, is Tx=33T_x = 33 tokens. (Em-dashes are punctuation and are not counted as tokens in this stylised scheme; the commas are also not separately tokenised.) A clinician reads this sentence and asks the model the question: "According to this letter, does the patient have atrial fibrillation, yes or no?"

Answer the three sub-questions below in order.

Sub-question 1 — identify the long-range dependency

State the two words (with their position indices) that the model must connect in order to answer the clinician's question correctly, and explain in one sentence why the connection between those two specific words is the one that matters — rather than, say, atrial7_{7} and paroxysmal32_{32}.

Sub-question 2 — RNN traversal cost

Assume the model is the RNN of ch. 06, with recurrence a<t>=g(Waaa<t−1>+Waxx<t>+ba)for t=1,…,Tx.a^{<t>} = g(W_{aa} \, a^{<t-1>} + W_{ax} \, x^{<t>} + b_a) \qquad \text{for } t = 1, \ldots, T_x.

Starting from the hidden state at the position of the earlier of the two words you identified in sub-question 1, count exactly how many applications of the recurrence are needed to propagate the dependency forward to the hidden state at the position of the later word. Show the calculation explicitly (the two positions, the subtraction, the result).

Sub-question 3 — Transformer attention: the single matrix entry

Assume the model is the Transformer of this chapter, with self-attention attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(QK⊤dk)V\text{attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\!\left(\dfrac{Q K^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V

where Q,K,V∈ℝTx×dQ, K, V \in \mathbb{R}^{T_x \times d} are matrices of queries, keys and values, one row per token of the sentence.

State the single entry (i,j)(i, j) of the Tx×TxT_x \times T_x matrix QK⊤Q K^{\top} that carries the long-range dependency you identified (give both row and column indices, using the convention that row ii is the query of position ii and column jj is the key of position jj). Then state, in one sentence, how many operations the dependency takes through the attention matrix — and how that count changes if the same two words were placed at the two extreme ends of a sentence of length Tx=1000T_x = 1000 instead of Tx=33T_x = 33.

Expected answer

Sub-question 1

The two words are rules5_{5} (or equivalently the bigram rules out5–6_{5\text{–}6}, but a single position is sufficient) and demonstrates31_{31} (or equivalently the bigram demonstrates paroxysmal31–32_{31\text{–}32}).

"Rules" (position 55) is the verb that says no: the discharge summary asserts atrial fibrillation is excluded. "Demonstrates" (position 3131) is the verb that says yes: the Holter recording, attached as appendix C, contradicts the discharge summary by showing paroxysmal episodes. The clinician's question is "yes or no on atrial fibrillation?", and the correct answer requires the model to connect these two specific verbs and recognise that the later evidence overrides the earlier conclusion.

Why not atrial7_{7} and paroxysmal32_{32}: these two words are semantically related at the topic level (both refer to a kind of cardiac rhythm disturbance), but they are not the words that carry the logical contradiction. A model that links atrial7_{7} to paroxysmal32_{32} has identified the topic of the sentence but has missed who-contradicts-whom — and would answer the clinician's question incorrectly.

(A response that names rules out / contradicts-style words — even using out6_{6} instead of rules5_{5}, or paroxysmal32_{32} instead of demonstrates31_{31} — is acceptable, provided the negation–assertion asymmetry is correctly named.)

Sub-question 2

The RNN's hidden state at position 55 is a<5>a^{<5>}; the hidden state at position 3131 is a<31>a^{<31>}. To propagate the dependency forward from a<5>a^{<5>} to a<31>a^{<31>}, the recurrence

a<t>=g(Waaa<t−1>+Waxx<t>+ba)a^{<t>} = g(W_{aa} \, a^{<t-1>} + W_{ax} \, x^{<t>} + b_a)

must be applied for every t=6,7,8,…,31t = 6, 7, 8, \ldots, 31.

That is 31−5=𝟐𝟔31 - 5 = \mathbf{26} applications of the recurrence. (Equivalently: there are 2525 intermediate hidden states a<6>,a<7>,…,a<30>a^{<6>}, a^{<7>}, \ldots, a^{<30>} strictly between a<5>a^{<5>} and a<31>a^{<31>}, with a<31>a^{<31>} as the final destination.)

The teachable beat: each of these 2626 applications is one occasion for the signal of rules out to fade as it is mixed with the contributions of the intervening words (atrial, fibrillation, although, the, 48-hour, Holter, ..., afternoon). The longer the sentence, the more occasions for fade.

Sub-question 3

The single entry of QK⊤Q K^{\top} that carries the long-range dependency is (QK⊤)31,5(Q K^{\top})_{31, 5}: row 3131 is the query of demonstrates, column 55 is the key of rules. This entry is a scalar — a single inner product ⟨q31,k5⟩\langle q_{31}, k_5 \rangle — and after the row-wise softmax + multiplication by VV it contributes directly to the representation at position 3131.

The symmetric entry (QK⊤)5,31(Q K^{\top})_{5, 31} is also acceptable as the answer, since self-attention computes all pairwise inner products and either direction encodes the same pairing.

The dependency takes one operation through the attention matrix (one inner product / one matrix entry).

Crucial sensitivity property: if the same two words were placed at the two extreme ends of a sentence of length Tx=1000T_x = 1000 — i.e. at positions 11 and 10001000 — the entry (QK⊤)1000,1(Q K^{\top})_{1000, 1} would still be a single entry, computed by a single inner product, in the same one operation. The RNN's count would explode from 2626 to 999999; the Transformer's count would stay at 11. That is the chapter's central architectural claim — the dependency between position 11 and position TxT_x is one operation away in the Transformer, regardless of how large TxT_x is.
