## Modalità duale — dove si attiva

Il riquadro **5** è il perno attivo: lì gira il sommativo "**Catch the overfitter**" (vedi [objectives.md](objectives.md)). L'artefatto condiviso è la **tabella a 4 modelli** (proiettata + distribuita) + un doc/lavagna a **4 colonne per gruppo** (*overfitter id + reason* · *winner id by CV* · *why CV ≠ test estimate* · *roles of the three subsets*). Un poll Slido sull'**overfitter id** (4 opzioni $M_A$/$M_B$/$M_C$/$M_D$) dà al docente un segnale aggregato e immediato anche dai remoti. Riquadri 1–4 espositivi (~20′), riquadro 5 ospita i ~10′ di sommativo (min 20–30), riquadro 6 chiude e fa ponte (~1–2′ alla fine, sovrapposto al min 30).

## Prompt visivi

Prompt pronti per un generatore di immagini. Lo **`style-anchor`** è definito *una volta* (stesso dei cap. 01, 02 e 03, per coerenza di serie tra capitoli) e va premesso a ogni prompt come parametro. Mai testo dentro l'immagine — le label numeriche/percentuali si aggiungono come overlay in slide-editor.

**`style-anchor`** = *"Coherent image set, one shared visual identity: warm orange (#E8741E) as the single accent colour on a dark-slate background, soft cinematic lighting, 16:9, crisp and high quality, no text, letters or numbers rendered anywhere in the image."*

- **Riquadro 1 (Hook)**: `style-anchor` + *a tight wiggly boundary that wraps every training dot with paper-thin precision, each dot in its own correctly-coloured small bubble; to the right a single new unfilled dot floats just outside the wrap in a mis-matched colour; a faint clinician silhouette in the background, expression of dismay.* **(Continuity callback to ch. 03 frame 6.)**
- **Riquadro 2 (Contesto)**: `style-anchor` + *split-panel diagram: left, a 2D scatter of two-colour patient dots each enclosed in its own correct-colour bubble (the model fitting training data perfectly); right, the same wiggly model overlaid on a new batch of unfilled dots, many of which fall on the wrong-colour side of the boundary; small clinician silhouette icon in the corner with a tiny speech bubble.*
- **Riquadro 3 (Sfida/Dati)**: `style-anchor` + *a minimalist line plot with two curves: one falling smoothly and monotonically toward zero (labelled visually as the training-error curve); a second descending then reversing into a clear U-shape (the new-data-error curve); the X-axis suggests increasing model complexity left-to-right; the gap between the two curves widens past the U's minimum.*
- **Riquadro 4 (Nodo / contamination principle)**: `style-anchor` + *a single bright orange token enters a stylised gate / threshold; on the other side it emerges greyed-out and visibly stamped (a circular shape resembling a USED stamp, no readable letters); behind the gate, separated by a clean glass pane, a second bright orange token sits untouched, evidently reserved.*
- **Riquadro 5 (Metodo + summative perno)**: `style-anchor` + *top half: a single horizontal bar divided into three coloured segments of decreasing width (training, validation, test); the test segment has a small padlock icon over it; the validation segment is expanded below into five equal blocks with circular rotation arrows showing one block held out at each of five steps. Bottom half: a clean four-row table showing four rows, each row associated with two visually distinct numeric percentages, one row's right-side percentage circled.*
- **Riquadro 6 (Payoff + pre-hook ch. 05)**: `style-anchor` + *three vertical columns side by side, each showing a distinct decision boundary shape (a piecewise wiggly curve, a smooth curve, an axis-aligned staircase); one column is highlighted with a small accent ribbon (the winner); to the side, a faint percentage tag floats; in the deep background, an out-of-focus horizon emerges with a soft, hazy network of small connected nodes — many small circles connected by thin lines, suggesting layers of artificial neurons.* **(Continuity callback to ch. 03 frame 5; pre-hook to ch. 05.)**
